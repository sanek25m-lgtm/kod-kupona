How to assess a hotel or travel coupon

Compare the same dates, guest count, room type and cancellation terms. A lower price may be for a different rate plan.

What to check

  • Check eligible destinations, hotels and dates.
  • Check the minimum spend and maximum discount.
  • Check whether taxes, fees, baggage and other required services are included.
  • Check whether the discount remains after choosing a payment method.
  • Check what happens to the discount if you change or cancel the booking.

Compare the final total at the last checkout step. There is no universal purchase time that guarantees the lowest price. Treat a code as valid only after the booking service confirms it.
