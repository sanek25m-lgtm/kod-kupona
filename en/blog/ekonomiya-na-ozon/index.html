How to check your savings on Ozon

Advertised discounts do not always equal actual savings. First choose the exact product: model, package, seller and quantity.

Calculate the final cost

Note the cart total including delivery to your address. Then apply an available code and compare the result. Count future purchase rewards separately from today's discount.

Check restrictions

An offer may be limited to new customers, a category, an app or a payment method. Check card payment and subscription terms on the marketplace itself; they change.

Before payment

Confirm that the code was accepted, the total decreased, and delivery and order contents stayed the same. If the discount did not apply, do not count it as savings. This guide does not promise any particular valid promo code.
