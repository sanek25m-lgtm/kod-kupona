How to use a grocery delivery promo code

Set your delivery address in the store service before choosing an offer. Availability may depend on your city, delivery area and account.

Check the terms

  • Check whether the offer is for a first or repeat order.
  • Check the minimum spend and which products count towards it.
  • Check category, time and payment restrictions.
  • Compare delivery costs before and after applying the code.

Applying the code

Copy the code exactly, add eligible items to your cart and enter it in the promo code field. Use the confirmed final total. This guide does not confirm the validity of any specific Pyaterochka or other store code.

The terms and conditions are translated automatically. Check the terms and conditions on the store's website.
