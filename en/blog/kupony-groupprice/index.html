How to use a coupon

  1. Open the offer card and check its expiry date, eligible products, minimum spend and other listed restrictions.
  2. Follow the link in the offer card to the store. If the offer includes a promo code, apply it as instructed; deals without a separate code may have different terms.
  3. Before paying, make sure the store applied the discount and compare the final total including delivery.
