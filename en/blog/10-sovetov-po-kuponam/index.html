10 tips for using coupons

  1. Choose the product you need first, then look for a discount.
  2. Check the expiry in the store's stated time zone.
  3. Check first or repeat order requirements.
  4. Compare the minimum spend with your cart contents.
  5. Read the list of excluded products and brands.
  6. Copy the code exactly: do not translate it or replace similar characters.
  7. Check whether it can be combined with other promotions.
  8. Include delivery and mandatory fees in the final price.
  9. Do not equate future rewards with money saved today.
  10. Before paying, confirm that the discount has applied.
