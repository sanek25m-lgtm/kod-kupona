How to compare final prices on Wildberries and Ozon

No single marketplace is the best value for every purchase. Compare the exact same item at the same time for the same address.

What to record

  • Model, size, quantity and package contents.
  • Seller and return terms.
  • Price after the confirmed discount.
  • Delivery charges and additional fees.
  • Payment method requirements.

A simple calculation

If the product costs 5 000 ₽, the confirmed discount is 10%, and the delivery is 200 ₽, the total is 4 700 ₽: 5 000 − 500 + 200. Compare this amount with the second platform. The example is hypothetical, this is not the result of checking current prices.

This article does not study 50 products or claim that one marketplace is always cheaper.
