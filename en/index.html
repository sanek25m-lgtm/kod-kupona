❓ How to use promo codes

Where do I enter a promo code?

Find the “Promo code” or “Discount code” field on the store website. Usually on the checkout page near the order total.

Why does the code not work?

Check: 1) coupon expiry; 2) the minimum order amount; 3) whether your item is eligible. If the issue persists — contact the store's support.

How many times can one code be used?

Usually 1 use per customer. Some codes can be used multiple times — this is stated in the coupon description.

How do I know a coupon is current?

Dates and terms come from the affiliate feed. This is not a checkout test; confirm the discount on your order before payment.
