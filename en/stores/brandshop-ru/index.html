BRANDSHOP loyalty program

The BRANDSHOP store loyalty program operates according to a savings system and implies the provision of a personal discount, the size of which depends on the amount of your savings. Discount 5% - amount of purchases from 50 000 to 99 999 ₽ Discount 10% - amount of purchases from 100 000 to 199 999 ₽ Discount 15% - the amount of purchases is more than 200 000 ₽ To receive a personal discount, you must apply for a loyalty card by filling out a form on the website or at the checkout of one of the stores. When applying for a savings card, you will need to indicate your full name, email address and date of birth.

How to use promotional codes and promotions

  1. Check if there is a separate promotional code on the card.
  2. For a promotional code, click “Show code and go to store”, copy the code and paste it into the cart.
  3. For a promotion without a code, open the store and check that the desired product or rate is included in the offer.
  4. Check the deadline, minimum order amount, exceptions, delivery and total payment.
